# Welcome to the Robot Tutor Experiment! 🤖📚

Hi there! Thanks for helping us test our new **Robot Tutor**. We want to see if a computer can teach you as well as a human teacher can.

This guide will explain exactly what you need to do. Don't worry, it's not a test for school! We are testing the **robot**, not you.

---

## Step 1: The "Before" Quiz (Pre-Test) 📝
First, we'll ask you a few questions to see what you already know.
*   **Why?** So we can measure how much you learn later.
*   **Don't worry:** It's okay if you don't know the answers yet! Just guess if you're not sure.

## Step 2: Learning with the Robot 🤖
You will spend about 20-30 minutes learning a new topic with our AI Tutor.
*   The robot will give you short lessons to read or watch.
*   It will ask you questions.
*   **Type your answers:** You can type full sentences! The robot is smart enough to understand you (mostly).
*   **It adapts to you:** If you get stuck, the robot might give you an easier question or a hint. If you are doing great, it might let you skip ahead!

## Step 3: The "After" Quiz (Post-Test) 🎓
After the lesson, you'll take another short quiz.
*   **Why?** We compare this score to your first quiz to calculate your **"Knowledge Gain"** (how much smarter you got!).

## Step 4: Tell Us What You Think (The Survey) 🗣️
This is the most important part! We need to know how you *felt*.

### 1. Was it hard to think? (NASA-TLX) 🧠
We will ask questions like:
*   "Did you have to think really hard?"
*   "Did you feel rushed?"
*   "Did you feel frustrated or annoyed?"
*   **Tip:** Be honest! If the robot was annoying, tell us.

### 2. Was the robot a good teacher? (SUS) 👍👎
We will ask questions like:
*   "Was the system easy to use?"
*   "Did the robot act weird or confusing?"
*   "Would you want to use this again?"

---

## 3 Big Groups
You will be in one of these three groups (but we won't tell you which one until the end!):
1.  **Group A (The Standard):** You get a normal lesson, just like a regular online class.
2.  **Group B (The Smart Robot):** You get our new AI that tries to personalize the lesson just for you.
3.  **Group C (The Human):** A real human teacher will be teaching you (secretly!).
